## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is a quiet, nostalgic folk-modern illustration: rounded simplified forms, a little naivety, flat perspective, symbolic details, gouache, crayon grain and dry-brush on paper. Keep the subject small inside abundant warm ivory whitespace. Derive two to four powdery pastel colours from the source—often dusty blue, ivory, plant green, with a small coral accent. It is not a photographic filter, a filled realistic scene, a smooth vector, a 3D plastic render, a high-saturation candy palette or a commercial title stack.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.
